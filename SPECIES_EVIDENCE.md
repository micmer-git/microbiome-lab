# Species evidence notes

Updated 2026-08-04. These notes document the primary study used for each of the 20 species cards. The cards deliberately distinguish a biochemical capacity, an observational human association, a preclinical experiment and a human intervention. A species name is never treated as a universal health label: strain, dose, substrate, host and community context can reverse the interpretation.

## Interpretation rules used in the interface

- “Human intervention” is reserved for an assigned exposure, not a cross-sectional abundance association.
- “Association” never becomes a causal verb such as “improves”, “protects” or “causes”.
- “Preclinical” means cell, organoid, ex-vivo or animal evidence and is stated in the card.
- “Strain-specific” findings are not generalized to total species abundance.
- Metabolite-production capacity is not treated as measured metabolite flux in a visitor.
- Neuroactive chemistry is not equated with a mood effect; gut tyramine, GABA or dopamine production does not demonstrate delivery to the brain.
- Food-driven relative-abundance change is not equated with administration of live or pasteurized organisms.
